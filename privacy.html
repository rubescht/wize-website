4SOS scam help (Golden Shield)

Our SOS scam help lets you describe what happened and receive general next steps from an A.I. assistant, and request an urgent call back from our team.

  • Messages you type are sent to an A.I. service provider to generate a reply. Before they are sent, we automatically remove anything that looks like a card, bank account or identity number.
  • Please do not enter card numbers, passwords, one-time codes or other sensitive details.
  • If you request a call back, we collect your name, phone number, preferred time and any short description you choose to give, and share it with our team so we can call you.
  • To protect the service from automated abuse, we use a short browser check and limit how often it can be used from one connection.
  • The assistant provides general guidance only. It is not legal or financial advice. In an emergency, call 000.
