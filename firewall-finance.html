Questions

Firewall finance, answered.

Who can apply?

Australian businesses can apply. Finance is available to approved customers only, subject to grenke's credit assessment and terms.

Is there really nothing to pay upfront?

Yes. Approved customers pay $0 upfront. Your first payment is part of your regular monthly schedule.

What does lease to own mean?

You make fixed monthly payments over an agreed term. When the term ends, ownership of the firewall passes to you.

Who supports the firewall?

Midnight IT handles installation, changes and faults, and Strike Force monitors security. You get your engineers' direct mobile numbers, plus Midnight IT after hours.

Can I finance it with Wize internet and phones?

Yes. Code Black Group offers payment plans with grenke across its services, so you can bundle your firewall with connectivity and phones. Your quote shows exactly what's included.

We already have a firewall. Can you help?

Yes. We'll check what you have, tell you honestly whether it needs replacing, and quote either way.
